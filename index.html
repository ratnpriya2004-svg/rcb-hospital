01 — About Us

A commitment to clinical excellence and community care.

RCB Hospital was established to address the critical need for dependable, dignified, and technologically sound healthcare in the Aliganj and Bausi corridor of Bhagalpur. We bring together certified clinical consultation, comprehensive diagnostic pathology, and prompt emergency response under a single, patient-centered roof.

Our clinical facility is purposefully engineered to eliminate common logistical hassles for patient families. By combining computerized testing laboratories with multi-department doctor suites, patients receive same-day evaluation without having to travel across multiple diagnostic kiosks.
